OmniSpeed Diagnostics // 03 What is Bufferbloat and Loaded Latency on the OmniSpeed Benchmark?
Bufferbloat occurs when home routers or ISP hardware queue excessive packets instead of transmitting them immediately. While an idle ping might report 6ms, heavy video streaming or downloads can cause loaded latency to spike into hundreds of milliseconds, leading to sudden gaming disconnects and stuttering voice calls. OmniSpeed measures loaded latency specifically to hold ISPs accountable.
OmniSpeed Diagnostics // 04 Why does OmniSpeed default to Multi-stream connection mode?
Multi-stream mode utilizes multiple concurrent TCP sockets to completely saturate high-bandwidth fiber and broadband pipes. Single-socket transfers are artificially throttled by TCP window scaling across long-distance transit tunnels. Multi mode reflects actual real-world conditions like multiple smart TVs, game downloads, and background syncs running together.
