The Three Dirty Tricks ISPs Use on Speed Tests

How Internet Service Providers (FLOW, Digicel, Comcast, Spectrum) manipulate consumer speed tests to claim "the problem is on your end."

01
DSCP 46 / EF Fast-Lanes

QoS Packet Whitelisting

When you visit Speedtest.net, your ISP's edge routers detect TCP port 8080 traffic directed to known Ookla IP blocks. The router instantly upgrades those packets into the Expedited Forwarding (EF) queue—bypassing the congested traffic pools where your actual video streams and downloads suffer.

02
Zero-Hop On-Net Routing

The Local Server Mirage

Speedtest.net automatically connects you to an on-net server hosted inside your own ISP's neighborhood exchange. This only measures the short copper/fiber line down your street—completely hiding the saturated transit links, subsea cables, and tier-1 backbones where your real connection crawls.

03
B2B Carrier Licensing

The Advertising Conflict of Interest

Ookla's parent company derives multimillion-dollar enterprise licensing revenue by selling Speedtest Intelligence® data and NetGauge licenses back to the telecoms. They have no incentive to build automated legal dispute dossiers that force ISPs to refund subscribers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Speedtest.net tell me I have 50 Mbps when a 500 MB file takes 15 minutes to download?
Speedtest.net uses multi-socket bursts to an on-net server prioritized by your ISP's QoS routing. In contrast, real downloads from GitHub, Steam, Google Drive, or Work VPNs traverse public peering nodes where your ISP actively shapes and chokes bandwidth. OmniSpeed measures real sustained HTTP/2 throughput across public transit paths.
Can my ISP refuse to give me a bill credit?
Customer service front-line reps are trained to say "speeds are up to and not guaranteed." However, under statutory consumer laws and utility licenses, persistent deficits (e.g. delivering 15 Mbps on a contracted 100 Mbps fiber line) constitute a material breach of contract. Presenting an official timestamped audit ledger and threatening regulatory complaint triggers escalation to retention managers authorized to credit accounts.
Is the OmniSpeed Background CLI Probe free?
Yes. You can run the hourly background probe directly in your browser tab for free, or download our open-source Python CLI daemon (omnispeed_probe.py) to run headlessly on a home server, Raspberry Pi, or laptop 24/7.
